# Candidate catalog sources (researched 2026-10-04)

What each candidate source could give Squirrelcade's catalogs (note 16 of the owner's review: other regions' releases, smaller North American physical releases, and proof that a game had a physical release), how it can be read, and what its terms allow. Checked on 2026-10-04 from the sources' own pages; terms change, so check again before building on one.

| Candidate | Covers | Access | Terms | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Redump** (discs) | PS1 to PS5, PSP, Xbox to Series X, GameCube, Wii, Wii U, Dreamcast, Saturn; physical discs by region, with serials; no release dates | DAT files per system, no key. libretro's copy on GitHub (CC BY-SA 4.0) has the older systems, not PS4, PS5, Xbox One or Series | Metadata declared public domain; its robots.txt keeps crawlers off the DAT downloads | Not now (below) |
| **No-Intro** (cartridges) | NES to 3DS, Atari, Sega and more, by region; digital sets are separate; no dates | DAT-o-MATIC (a form), or libretro's copy on GitHub | Free reuse, no attribution needed (its terms page, updated 2026-09-11) | Not now (below) |
| **GameTDB** | Wii, GameCube, Wii U, DS, 3DS, Switch, PS3; marks retail cartridges and discs apart from downloads; regions, dates | A zip or text file per console, no key | Its FAQ lets anyone use it in projects; no data licence; robots.txt asks crawlers to keep off the files | Possible later, read only when the owner asks |
| ScreenScraper | 300+ systems | API with developer credentials and quotas | Free apps only, with approval; data CC BY-NC-SA | Not usable |
| TheGamesDB | All platforms, no physical marker | Key after login; a JSON dump | No data licence found | Not usable |
| Giant Bomb | Releases by region and platform | Free key, 200 requests an hour | Non-commercial (not verified: its site blocks automated reading) | Not usable |
| MobyGames | Release dates by platform and country | Paid API | Credit, no redistribution | Not usable |
| Publishers' shops (Super Rare Games, Limited Run Games, Strictly Limited Games, Play-Asia) | Their own releases | Shop pages only | Super Rare Games' and Limited Run Games' terms forbid copying or scraping; Play-Asia blocks automated reading | Not usable |
| **Wikipedia's pages of [Super Rare Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Rare_Games) and [Limited Run Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Limited_Run_Games_releases) releases** | Super Rare Games' numbered releases by console (199); Limited Run Games' releases (809 across 19 consoles) | Web page tables | CC BY-SA | **Add as online lists** (0.42.0 reads both layouts) |
| Redump's wiki lists of undumped discs by region | PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Wii U releases, with serials, marked incomplete | Web page tables | No licence stated | Possible as online lists, at the owner's choice |

**Why no disc or cartridge database now.** Measured on the owner's collection: of the 430 games waiting as "Not confirmed physical", Redump's and No-Intro's lists (libretro's copy) would confirm 37 (29 of them on the PlayStation). Most of the rest are accessories, test cartridges, demo discs and bundles, which no game list has, and on the newer consoles mostly announced games and special editions, which IGDB settles as they come out. Neither database covers PS4, PS5, Xbox One or Series through a source that may be read automatically.

**Why the two Wikipedia pages.** Of the owner's copies in "Not in catalog" on the newer consoles, 102 are on them (99 of 160 on the Switch: the PAL limited prints such as Dorfromantik, Unrailed and Haiku, the Robot). Added with their games counting once owned, those copies find their games without adding hundreds of missing ones.
