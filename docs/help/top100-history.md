# Top 100 and history

With **Top 100 lists and console history** on (Settings > Features; it is by default), each console's page has two more tabs, and Platforms has a timeline.

## Top 100

A console's best games in ranked order: a consensus of critics' and retrospective best-of lists, for 45 consoles, that comes with Squirrelcade. For each game:

- its cover, year, developer and publisher (from IGDB);
- **why it matters**, a sentence or two, where Squirrelcade has one;
- **what you have of it:** owned (with each copy's condition: CIB, sealed, loose...), to review (a copy of yours may be it: answer on the Review page), or not owned (with the other consoles or PC storefronts you have it on, its place on your wishlist and your preference);
- a link to it in [RomM](romm.md), when that's on.

The top line sums it up ("Own 63 of 100 · 58 of them in RomM"). The list opens as a **board** of ten by ten: each square a game, its box art in the middle and its rank in the corner, filled green when you own it (the art in color), a dashed gold square when you don't (the art in gray), with a dot when it's in RomM; a square opens its game. On a phone the squares show the rank alone. Beside it, the **hunting list**: the games you don't have, best first, with where each one is (on PC, on another console, on your wishlist) and your preference. **List** shows the list itself with why each game matters. Filter to the games you own or don't own, or to the ones in RomM: on the board the others dim.

Whether you own a listed game follows your console's catalog, so your Review answers and mappings count; a game the catalog doesn't have counts by its title. Another region's console of its own counts with its console: a Super Famicom copy counts on the Super Nintendo's list, and the Super Famicom's page shows the same list.

## History

A console's **History** tab: who made it, its generation, its launches by region with their prices, units sold and what it competed with (as tiles), its launches and end along a line, key hardware, how it did and why, its firsts and its end, and **Start here**: a handful of games that show what the console was about, with whether you have them.

Some consoles have all of it; for the others, the facts, so far.

## Drafts and sources

The history texts, and why each game matters, were drafted with Claude's help from Wikipedia. Every text shows its sources and whether it's a **Draft** (not checked yet) or **Verified**. Figures that change over time say when they're from ("Figures as of 2026-09").

As the owner, you can:

- mark a console's history verified (its **...** menu) or a game's text (**Edit** under it, **Verified**);
- rewrite either, or write one for a console or game that has none (**Write its history**, **Write why it matters** in a game's drawer);
- go **Back to Squirrelcade's** text.

Your version is kept apart from Squirrelcade's. If a later version of Squirrelcade changes its own text, yours stays, with a note offering the new one.

## Timeline

**Platforms > Timeline:** the console generations side by side, then year by year, each console's launch and its landmark games (the best of its Top 100, and its Start here games), marked ✓ when you have them. It shows the consoles you collect; **Consoles you don't collect too** shows them all. How many games per console is a setting (Settings > Interface > "Landmark games per console on the timeline").

## In the game drawer

A game on a Top 100 list shows its rank ("#1 in the console's Top 100") and why it matters.
