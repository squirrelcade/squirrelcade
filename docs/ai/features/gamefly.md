## What it is

GameFly (a rental service that also sells games) lists new and used prices. With this on, Squirrelcade shows GameFly's prices beside the wishlist's games on the consoles it sells. It needs no account and no key: it reads GameFly's public pages, and only for the wishlist's games.

## Have ready

- Nothing.

## Steps

1. In Squirrelcade: **Settings > Sources > GameFly**: turn on **GameFly's prices on the wishlist**.
2. Optional: **How many wishlist games** and **Read the prices again every** in the same card; the starting values are fine.

## Check that it works

- **System > Status > Integrations:** the GameFly light is green after its first run (**Check now** runs it at once).
- **Acorns > Acorns wishlist:** games on the consoles GameFly sells show its new and used prices.

## How long it takes

about 2 minutes; give it 5. The second number is on purpose: setting up outside accounts usually takes longer than it looks, and nobody should feel behind.

## If something goes wrong

- Red: GameFly changed its pages or didn't answer; **Check now** later. Help > Integrations (GameFly) says more.
