# About this data

`history.json` holds the console histories, "Start here" picks and "why it matters" texts that ship with Squirrelcade. They were drafted with AI help from Wikipedia articles, which are published under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each text keeps its sources (the Wikipedia articles it came from) beside it, and the app shows them. These texts are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0, apart from Squirrelcade's code (AGPL-3.0). They're marked as drafts until checked.

`top100.json` holds each console's ranked Top 100: a consensus of critics' and retrospective best-of lists, with a short reason for each game. The rankings are facts and opinions gathered from those lists; the reasons follow the same terms as the histories.

Squirrelcade isn't affiliated with Wikipedia, the Wikimedia Foundation or any of the publications its lists draw on.
